Emergency Relief

Standing with Flood-Affected Assam

The recent floods across Assam have left thousands of families in Jorhat and Sivasagar districts without food, clean water, or basic essentials. Pure for Cure — together with Hare Krishna Mandir North Guwahati — is on the ground distributing emergency relief kits to families in the worst-affected areas.

Each Relief Kit Contains

One kit provides a flood-affected family with essential groceries to cook meals at home.

  • Rice 3 kg
  • Wheat Flour (Atta) 1 kg
  • Dal 1 kg
  • Soyabean 200 g
  • Mustard Oil 1 litre
  • Salt 1 kg
  • Turmeric Powder 200 g
